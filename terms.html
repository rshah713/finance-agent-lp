Last updated: September 6, 2026

These terms govern use of Finance Agent, a Google Cloud application and LLM tool that reads a student organization’s official budget Google Sheet and files receipt evidence in Google Drive. By authorizing or using the application, you agree to these terms.

The service

Finance Agent is intended to be called as a tool by a language-model assistant. It lists events and line items already present on the organization’s budget workbook and appends receipt log rows with a Drive link. Google Sheets and Google Drive remain the systems of record. We keep zero data on our servers. The application does not replace treasurer judgment, OSIL review, or NJIT financial policy.

Eligibility and authorization

The application is for authorized members of the student organization that operates it, typically through an LLM assistant that invokes Finance Agent as a tool. You may use it only with credentials and Google files you are permitted to access. You must follow NJIT acceptable-use and Workspace rules.

Your responsibilities

  • Upload only genuine receipts or supporting documents for organization purchases.
  • Do not use the application to alter, hide, or falsify financial records.
  • Do not grant the application access to personal Google files unrelated to club finance.
  • Understand that attaching a receipt is evidence, not approval to change planned or actual amounts.
